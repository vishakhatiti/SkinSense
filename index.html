About the Project

SkinSense is a web-based recommendation system developed under AyurCare Tech that helps users identify the most suitable organic soap and herbal shampoo based on skin and hair concerns.

Organic soaps are recommended as the primary solution, while herbal shampoo is suggested only when hair fall or dandruff is selected.
